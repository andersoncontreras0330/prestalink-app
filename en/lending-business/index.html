Your lending business, with your whole team

PrestaLink Business is for lenders who no longer collect alone: each employee signs in with their own account, sees what is theirs and everything is recorded under their name.

Collection route and daily cash

The collector opens their route with the latest payers first, and calls, collects or notes what happened. What they collect goes into their cash on its own; at the end of the day they count it and send the cash count, and you see whether it matches.

  • How much there is to lend

    The business's capital, in cash and in its accounts, goes up when you collect and down when you hand over a loan. Business control shows how much you made, how much you can lend and what happens if you lend more, with expenses and each collector's commission.

  • Partners

    Record the capital each partner put in, the rate it is lent at and what you agreed to pay them. The app reminds you of payment dates, and an invited partner sees only their capital and what they are owed. It is a record of your agreement with them: PrestaLink doesn't offer investments or guarantee returns.

  • Pawn shop

    Items with their ticket, photos, appraisal, renewals, redemptions and sales. With the price per gram of gold, the app suggests the appraisal from weight and karats.

  • Branches

    Up to 3 active branches, each with its team and clients.

A loan above the limit of whoever records it waits until someone with permission approves it. What each person does stays in the activity log, which can't be edited or deleted.

Price

US$15 per month. It includes the owner, 3 employees and everything in the lender subscription. Only the owner pays: employees and partners join for free, by invitation.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to enter my clients again?

No. The in-house clients you already have move to the business with their loans and payments, and you can also import them from Excel.

What if an employee leaves?

You remove their access and they stop seeing the business. What they recorded stays, under their name.

Does PrestaLink hold the business's money?

No. Cash, accounts and capital are records kept by your business: PrestaLink doesn't receive, hold or move money.
