Web purchases

In the Android app downloaded from this website you pay by card: lenders, for the PrestaLink Pro subscription or, if they run a business with staff, PrestaLink Business; borrowers, if they want it, for the PrestaLink Plus subscription.

Plans

PrestaLink Pro (lenders): monthly, US$9.99 per month; yearly, US$79.99 per year. Lender accounts get 30 days free from when they are created, and you are only charged if you subscribe.

PrestaLink Business (the owner of a lending business or pawn shop): monthly, US$15 per month. It includes everything in Pro, the owner and 3 employees, and up to 3 branches. Only the owner pays.

PrestaLink Plus (borrowers, optional): monthly, US$3 per month, with no free trial. It lets you post what you need to receive offers from lenders and see the lowest rate nearby; offers and loans are not guaranteed.

Before paying you see the total, including your country's taxes.

Who charges you

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for all web purchases. Paddle charges you, issues the receipt and handles questions about charges and refunds.

Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period. You can cancel it at any time from the link in the email Paddle sends you, and access continues until the end of the paid period.

Refunds

If you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your first web payment. Write to us at soporte@prestalink.app (or prestalink.soporte@gmail.com) or request it at paddle.net. This does not limit any rights your country's law gives you.
