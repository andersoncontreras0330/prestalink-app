How each type is calculated

The same loan can cost more or less depending on how interest is charged. This is a US$10,000 loan at 2% monthly over 5 months, calculated six ways:

TypeHow it is paidTotal interestTotal
Fixed loan5 installments of US$2,200.00US$1,000.00US$11,000.00
Level installment (bank)5 installments of US$2,121.58US$607.92US$10,607.92
Principal paymentsUS$2,000 + interest, each monthUS$600.00US$10,600.00
Interest-only4 of US$200.00, principal at the endUS$1,000.00US$11,000.00
Single paymentAll of it after 150 daysUS$1,000.00US$11,000.00
Running interestInterest each month, principal when repaidUS$1,000.00US$11,000.00

The fixed loan and the level installment use the same rate, but not the same interest: the fixed loan always charges it on the original US$10,000, and the level installment on what is left. See how to calculate loan interest step by step.

Yearly or monthly rate?

The calculator converts the rate by how many periods there are in a year: 12 months, 24 fifteen-day periods, 52 weeks or 365 days. So 24% yearly is 2% monthly and 1% every 15 days. Choose the period your rate was quoted in and, separately, how often it is paid.

Will it match my bank?

"Level installment" gives you the principal and interest payment. Banks usually add insurance, commissions and fees that are not included here, so the one in your contract may be higher.

The rates in these examples are only examples: PrestaLink doesn't set or recommend rates. Your country's law may cap them.
