Keep track of your loans and collections

What to write down for every loan so the accounts add up, and how PrestaLink does it for you.

What to write down for every loan

Most trouble between a lender and a borrower comes from something that was never written. This is the minimum:

  • Who and how much: the person's name and contact, the amount and the currency.
  • The rate and its period: "5%" means nothing unless you know if it is per month, per week or for the whole loan.
  • How it is paid: in installments, on a single date, or only the interest while the principal is returned. And how often.
  • The handover date and the first payment date: they decide how much interest goes in the first installment.
  • The late fee: whether there is one, how much, and after how many grace days. See how to calculate a late fee.
  • Every payment: the date you received the money, which is not always the day you record it, and how much went to interest and to principal.

Getting started with PrestaLink

  1. Create your lender account. You get 30 days free.
  2. Bring what you already have. Import clients and loans from Excel or CSV, or add them by hand or by voice.
  3. Record every payment. The app splits what you receive: late fees first, then interest, then principal. If they pay extra, the following installments go down.
  4. Check your portfolio. How much you have out, how much you earned, who is late and what is due in the next 30 days.

Clients who don't use the app

Your clients don't need to install anything: you record them as in-house clients and only you see their data. If one wants to see their installments, share your code; they open a free account and you both see the same thing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I fix a payment I recorded wrong?

On a loan to an in-house client you can delete the last payment and record it again.

What if they pay less than the installment?

Record it under "Did they pay a different amount?": it covers the interest first and then the principal, and whatever is left stays pending.

Can I take my data out?

Yes. You can export your portfolio to a spreadsheet, with your loans or with every transaction.
