About us

Loans between people, with clear rules.

What PrestaLink is

PrestaLink is a ledger for loans between people who already know each other. We are not the lender and we don't find lenders: we don't put up the money, we don't charge interest and we don't decide who gets a loan. What we do is make sure the terms are written down before the money changes hands, and that the app, not each person's memory, keeps track of what is owed.

Who it is for

If you lend

Clients with an account and clients who don't use the app, in the same list. Share your code and they send you their request.

Loan tracking

If you run a business

Each employee with their own account: collection routes, cash, approvals, branches and a pawn shop.

PrestaLink Business

If you borrowed

You see the same accounts as your lender: installments, payments and what is left. It is free.

How it works

  1. You agree

    The amount, the rate, how it is paid and the late fee are written down before the money changes hands.

  2. The money is handed over

    The lender confirms it and the app builds the installments.

  3. Every payment is recorded

    With its date. The app splits it between late fees, interest and principal.

  4. You both see the same

    What is left and what comes next, if the client uses the app too.

What we don't do

  • We don't move money

    The two people hand over and pay on their own, and record it here.

  • We don't collect for anyone

    We don't guarantee loans or decide who gets one either.

  • We don't recommend rates

    Each lender sets their own.

Your data

Everyone sees only their own. One lender can't see another's portfolio, and a client can't see who else their lender lends to. This doesn't depend on the app but on the database, which rejects any query for data that isn't yours.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

It is free for borrowers. Lender accounts get 30 days free, and then the subscription is US$9.99 per month or US$79.99 per year. PrestaLink Business is US$15 per month. If the subscription lapses, you can still see your loans and record payments.

Does PrestaLink lend money?

No. PrestaLink doesn't lend, doesn't move money and doesn't decide who gets a loan. It is where two people write down what they agreed and keep the accounts.

Do my clients have to install the app?

No. You can record clients who don't use it (in-house clients), with their loans and payments. If one of them installs it, they see their installments and what they still owe.

Does it work for weekly or biweekly payments?

Yes. Payments can be daily, weekly, every 15 days, monthly or yearly, and the rate can use a different period: for example, a monthly rate with weekly payments. The minimum term for a loan is 15 days.

Which currencies does it support?

Dominican peso, US dollar, Colombian peso, Mexican peso, Argentine peso, Chilean peso, Peruvian sol, euro and Venezuelan bolívar. Each currency is added up separately: the app never makes up exchange rates.

Is it available for iPhone and Android?

Yes. The iPhone app is on the App Store. The Android app is downloaded from this page.
